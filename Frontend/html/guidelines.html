Warehouse Equipment Simulators

Two browser-based 3D simulations built to support our health & safety training programme. Each one walks through a real operating sequence and coaches you on technique as you go. They run directly in the browser — nothing to install, no account needed.

Device: desktop or laptop recommended (keyboard controls)
Browser: Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari
Sound: on, for horn and feedback cues
Module 01

Overhead Crane Handling

Operate a warehouse overhead gantry crane: lift a crate from the marked pickup zone, carry it clear of a striped hazard walkway, and set it down gently in the drop zone. The load swings like a real suspended load, so smooth, gradual movements are the whole point.

How it runs

  1. Read the briefing screen, then start the lift.
  2. Drive the trolley over the crate in Zone A and lower the hook until it's close enough to attach.
  3. Lift and carry the load toward Zone B, routing around the hazard zone — a load should never pass over it.
  4. Lower the load fully to the floor inside Zone B, then release.
  5. Review your score, time, and any safety flags on the results screen.

Controls

←↑↓→Move gantry / trolley
WSHoist up / down
SpaceAttach hook / release load
DragOrbit camera
ScrollZoom in / out
Assessed: load control, hazard avoidance, safe release, time.
Tip: sudden starts and stops cause the load to swing. Ease into every movement and let it settle before changing direction. Rule: you can't set a load down in the hazard zone, and you can't release it mid-air — it must be fully grounded first.

Module 02

Forklift Operator Handling

Walk through the real sequence of a counterbalance forklift job: a pre-operation walk-around check, then a guided drive where the sim coaches you live on seatbelt use, keeping the load low while travelling, sounding the horn at blind corners, and controlling your speed indoors.

How it runs

  1. Complete the pre-operation walk-around — tick off tyres, forks, mast, horn/lights, seatbelt, and fluid leaks before the engine will start.
  2. Buckle your seatbelt before moving off — the forklift won't accelerate without it.
  3. Drive out, approach a crate square-on, lower the forks, and pick it up.
  4. Carry it to the striped bay at a safe travel height, sounding the horn at the blind corner.
  5. Lower the forks and set the crate down inside the bay.
  6. Check your debrief: time on task and any safety flags raised.

Controls

WSDrive forward / reverse
ADSteer left / right
↑↓Raise / lower forks
ZXTilt mast back / forward
EPick up / set down crate
HSound horn
BBuckle / unbuckle seatbelt
Assessed: pre-op check, seatbelt use, travel height, speed, horn use.
Tip: live coaching appears the moment the sim notices risky technique — raised forks while moving, going too fast, or missing the horn at a corner. Each tip shows once per session.

Module 03

Workplace Ethics & Conduct

Safety starts with personal integrity, accountability, and respect for teammates. Nexsus Logistics upholds high standards of professional ethics across all facility operations.

Core Principles

  1. Incident Reporting: Immediately report all near-misses, injuries, and equipment damages within 2 hours. Concealing incidents is prohibited.
  2. Substance-Free Environment: Operating machinery under the influence of alcohol, illegal drugs, or impairing medication is strictly prohibited.
  3. Equipment Integrity: Never bypass safety interlocks, disable horns, or operate uncertified machinery.
  4. Team Safety: Speak up if a coworker is performing a dangerous maneuver. Maintain a clean, safe work floor.

Ethics Rulebook

ImpairmentZero tolerance policy; mandatory drug & alcohol testing.
Near-MissesMust be reported to shift supervisor promptly.
TamperingModifying safety governors or switches results in termination.
Module 04

Hazard Tasks & Safe Operating Procedures

Understanding hazard controls, correct ergonomics, chemical spill handling, and Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) is mandatory before stepping onto the warehouse floor.

Standard Safety Tasks

  1. Manual Lifting: Bend at the knees, keep heavy loads close to your chest, pivot with your feet—never twist your spine.
  2. Spill Response: Isolate chemical or oil spills, place warning cones, and notify the hazmat team immediately. Never touch unknown liquids.
  3. PPE Compliance: High-visibility vest, steel-toe boots, and hard hat must be worn at all times in active transport zones.
  4. Lockout / Tagout (LOTO): De-energize and tag malfunctioning machinery before inspection or cleaning.

Key Hazard Controls

PedestriansStay inside yellow walkways; maintain safe distance from operating forklifts.
Blind CornersStop, look, and sound horn before walking or driving into intersections.
Lifting LimitLoads over 25kg require 2-person lift or mechanical assistance.
