Who We Are

Built around the production line

We started as a two-warehouse operation supporting a single parts supplier. Today, Northbridge runs just-in-time delivery, storage and sequencing for a dozen automotive manufacturers and tier-one suppliers, moving components on schedules measured in minutes, not days.

Automotive lines don't stop for logistics problems — so we've built our business around not being one. That means precise inventory tracking, sequenced loading, and a workforce trained to spot and stop hazards before they become downtime.

What We Do

Three ways we keep production moving

01

Warehousing & Storage

Climate-controlled and high-bay storage sited close to major automotive plants, with inventory accuracy built into every pick and put-away.

02

JIT Sequencing

Parts arrive at the line in build order, not just on time — cutting the handling and searching that slows assembly down.

03

Freight Distribution

A dedicated fleet and trusted haulage partners connecting suppliers, our sites, and client plants on tight, repeatable schedules.
