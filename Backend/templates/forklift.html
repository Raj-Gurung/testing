Forklift Handling

Before You Start the Engine

This module walks through the real sequence of a counterbalance forklift job: a pre-operation walk-around, then a guided drive where the sim coaches you on technique — not just "drive here, lift that."

You'll be marked on things real operators are checked on: seatbelt use, keeping the load low while travelling, sounding the horn at blind corners, and controlling your speed indoors.

Pre-Operation Walk-Around

Check Every Item

A real operator never skips this. Tap each item once you've reviewed why it matters.

  • ✓TyresNo cuts, chunks, or flat spots — a damaged tyre can tip a load without warning.
  • ✓Forks & carriageNo cracks or bends, tips level, locking pins seated — an uneven fork drops loads sideways.
  • ✓Mast & chainsEven chain tension, no fraying — this is what holds the load up.
  • ✓Horn & lightsTest both — the horn is your warning at every blind corner.
  • ✓SeatbeltMust be worn any time the forklift is moving — it's what keeps you in the seat if it tips.
  • ✓Fluid leaksCheck the ground under the mast and engine for hydraulic or oil drips.
